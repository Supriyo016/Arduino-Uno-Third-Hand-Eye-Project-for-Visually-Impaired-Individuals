01 · TRANSMITTrigger the HC-SR04

The firmware sends a 10 microsecond trigger pulse to the sensor on D12.

02 · MEASURERead echo duration

Echo pulse time is captured on D10 with a 30 ms timeout, then converted to centimetres.

03 · ALERTActivate the buzzer

If the reading is valid and within the configured threshold, D5 goes HIGH briefly.
