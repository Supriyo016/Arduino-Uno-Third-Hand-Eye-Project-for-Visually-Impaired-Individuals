01 / SENSESend an ultrasonic pulse

The Arduino gives the HC-SR04 a 10 microsecond trigger pulse.

02 / CALCULATEConvert echo time to distance

Firmware uses microseconds ÷ 29 ÷ 2. A missing echo is treated as zero.

03 / RESPONDCheck the configured threshold

The buzzer predicate is true only when the measured distance is greater than zero and at or below the threshold.
